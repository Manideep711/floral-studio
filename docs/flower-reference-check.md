# Flower and tradition reference check

Tangedu is represented as Senna auriculata. The current 3D revision has five unequal petals, three larger gently curved lower stamens, four smaller stamens and three reduced upper stamens. It is a stylized model rather than a botanically exact specimen. The previous identical straight rods have been removed.
Reference: [Kew, Flora of Tropical East Africa description](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:518328-1/general-information). This documents the 3/4/3 stamen grouping; curvature is a restrained visual interpretation rather than a claim that all Senna filaments must be curved. [University of Florida](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/care/weeds-and-invasive-plants/cassia-confusion/) distinguishes shorter Senna stamens, which may be curved, from S-shaped Cassia stamens.

Gunugu uses the spike form of Celosia argentea, not the crested cockscomb form. White/ivory and soft pink fit the botanical descriptions. The plum and saturated pink palette entries remain explicitly labelled decorative; existing saved colours are preserved.
Reference: [Kew Celosia argentea](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:316350-2/general-information).

Banti uses a cultivated, full marigold head of Tagetes erecta, with yellow/orange colour choices. Cultivar appearance varies.
Reference: [Kew Tagetes erecta](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:252092-1/general-information).

The concentric, tapering gopuram form and seasonal flowers are supported by [Vikarabad district's festival description](https://vikarabad.telangana.gov.in/festival/bathukamma/). The user's family procedure describes a plate, crossed threads, leaf lining, pumpkin leaves, Tangedu base, radial Gunugu and a pumpkin flower at the peak. It explicitly allows substitutions and mentions coloured flowers. It must not be rewritten as a single universal rule forbidding all added colour.

[An ethnobotanical study](https://floraandfona.org.in/Uploaded%20Pdf/232/291-300.pdf) also describes a lotus/pumpkin flower and turmeric Gouramma at the top, and coloured Gunugu. The app's About copy now acknowledges these finishing elements are not yet represented. Wooden peetam placement, leaf lining, ties and Gouramma should inform a future traditional arrangement mode; they were not silently added to or enforced on the user's saved design. The three-species studio is a creative digital interpretation, not a complete ritual guide.

This revision reduces the key light, raises gentle fill, widens the PCF shadow filter and lightens the radial plate contact shadow. No extra postprocessing passes or dependencies. The 2D slider performance work is retained.

Validation: 32 tests pass, including full/mobile geometry and existing triangle budgets; production build/TypeScript pass. A mobile-sized browser viewer shows the revised lighter rendering. No physical-phone performance claim is made.
