# Research and asset provenance

Research checked 2026-10-09.

## Cultural sources

- Telangana Tourism, festivals and fairs: https://www.tourism.telangana.gov.in/page/festivals-and-fairs — names tangedu, gunuka, banti, chemanti, nandi-vardhanam and describes circular rows on a brass plate. Website direct fetch was intermittently unavailable; indexed text was accessible during blueprint research.
- Telangana Government, Language and Culture: https://www.telangana.gov.in/about/language-culture/ — stacked flowers, Tangedu/Gunugu/Chamanti, communal celebration.
- EPTRI–ENVIS, Bathukamma article: https://eptrienvis.nic.in/All%20PDF%20Files/8.%20Bathukamma%20-%20The%20Flowers%20Festival%20of%20Telangana%20Article.pdf — Celosia association, leaf/stem support, conical mound, colored Gunugu arrangements. Article itself identifies 2015; search crawl dates should not be mistaken for publication dates.

## Botanical sources

- Kew Senna auriculata: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A518328-1 — accepted name; Cassia auriculata synonym. Its photo gallery is a reference source, not copied imagery.
- eFloras Celosia argentea: https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=5&taxon_id=200006992 — dense tapered-to-cylindrical spike, silvery/pink appearance.
- NC State Extension Tagetes erecta: https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/tagetes-erecta/ — marigold morphology and color reference.

## Unresolved review items

- Confirm the locally appropriate Gunugu form with Telangana makers and real arrangement close-ups. Do not equate all Celosia forms with the spike shown here.
- Confirm native Telugu labels and transliterations (Tangedu/Thangedu, Gunugu/Gunuka, Banti/Banthi).
- Tagetes erecta is a selected Banti exemplar, not a universal identification for the common name.
- Regional/family practices vary. Five default layers are a design choice, not a traditional rule.
- The starter uses a labeled decorative pink for Gunugu; pale/pink natural colors are also available. Decorative colors do not imply natural botanical pigmentation.
- No sacred figure is included in this initial editor. A culturally reviewed crown treatment can be considered later.
- A maker-reviewed photograph sheet and flower asset review are still needed before release.

## Asset provenance

All floral vector paths are original project artwork generated in code. No reference photographs or externally authored 3D models are shipped. No AI image or paid API is used.

Noto Sans Telugu source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanstelugu — downloaded from the official repository; SIL OFL license bundled in public/fonts/OFL.txt.
